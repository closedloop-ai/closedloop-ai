### A new story is NOT done until `validate-story-titles.mjs` exits 0 (REQUIRED — or `storybook-build` fails)
The `storybook-build` CI job runs `apps/storybook/scripts/validate-story-titles.mjs`. It
checks each story title against the GENERATED catalog `packages/design-system/storybook/component-catalog.ts`.
**The catalog is NOT derived from your story's `Meta.title`** — it is generated by
`apps/storybook/scripts/sync-component-catalog.mjs` from the design-system component files,
a curated `designSystemCategoriesById` map, and a `designSystemPrivateSurfaceIds` exclusion
set. So `catalog:sync` will NOT invent an entry just because you wrote a story. Running it
and committing `component-catalog.ts` is only correct when you genuinely added a brand-new
PUBLIC, top-level cataloged surface (and then the title MUST equal the generated entry's
`storyTitle`, and the story file's `<id>` = filename stem must match the catalog `storyId`).

Before finishing, run `node apps/storybook/scripts/validate-story-titles.mjs` (must exit 0).
Classify each component you wrote a story for:
1. **Genuine new public surface** → ensure it gets a catalog entry (`catalog:sync`, add a
   `designSystemCategoriesById` category if needed), and title the story to the EXACT generated
   `storyTitle`. Commit the regenerated `component-catalog.ts`.
2. **Private primitive** (listed in `designSystemPrivateSurfaceIds`, e.g. `pagination`, `empty`)
   or a **duplicate/showcase gallery** of an already-cataloged surface → it is intentionally NOT
   cataloged. Do not force a standalone cataloged story; if a showcase story is desired, add its
   `storyId` to `ignoredStoryIds` in `validate-story-titles.mjs` WITH a one-line rationale.
3. **NEVER title a story to a cataloged surface while importing a DIFFERENT component.** The catalog
   entry names an exact `sourcePath`; the story for that `storyId` must import THAT component. The
   donut-chart trap: catalog `donut-chart` → `components/ui/donut-chart.tsx` (the `DonutDatum`
   Recharts chart), NOT `components/ui/primitives/donut-chart.tsx` (a separate public primitive).
   Titling a `primitives/donut-chart` story as the `donut-chart` surface silently mis-documents it
   and leaves the real `ui/donut-chart` uncovered. When two same-named public components exist,
   give the cataloged one the catalog title+sourcepath, and treat the other as case 1 or 2.
Never hand-edit `component-catalog.ts` (it is generated); `ignoredStoryIds` is the right home for
intentional exclusions.
